The two plans

  • Glow Up: the AI adds products to the customer's yard photo. Each item links to your Amazon store.
  • Glow Up Plus: a full makeover your company builds. The customer taps Get This Built to send you a quote request.

What's real here

  • The screens, buttons, wording, and item lists match the app code you'll open in Xcode.
  • The yard pictures are drawings so you can try the slider. In the real app, the after picture comes from the AI image model and looks like a photo of the customer's yard.
  • You can try your own photo. It stays in your browser. Since no AI is connected to this preview, the after picture is your original photo.
  • Once you add your product links, View on Amazon opens each product in your store. Share opens the iPhone share sheet. Here both show a short message instead.
  • Get This Built saves the customer's details and design on your server. Here nothing is sent.

Run the real app

Open the project in Xcode on a Mac and follow the README in the backyard-glowup folder.
